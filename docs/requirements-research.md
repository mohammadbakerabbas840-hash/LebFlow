# Lebanese Administrative Services: Requirements and Appointment Access

## Targeted update — 27 September 2026

- Rechecked [General Security's passport fees and processing page](https://www.general-security.gov.lb/ar/posts/493). Published base amounts remain LBP 6,000,000 for five years and LBP 10,000,000 for ten years; urgent handling adds a service charge. These are not all-inclusive quotes or live appointment availability.
- Added [DGCS ID-card guidance](https://www.dgcs.gov.lb/arabic/identitycard), including renewal with the original card and child applications. The page contains inconsistent age wording and a legacy stamp amount; neither is treated as a verified current rule/price. Confirm with the receiving mukhtar/civil-registry office.
- Added [DGCS electronic civil-status extracts](https://www.dgcs.gov.lb/arabic/ekhraj-al-kaid-electronic): first issuance at the place of registration, later copies possible at the place of residence. The page links an application form; complete supporting-document requirements and a current total fee remain unverified.
- Registration requirements are LebFlow account policy, not government eligibility: both names are requested, but ID number and uploads are optional. Parents can register without a child's existing ID/extract. Scans of optional account documents do not establish government authenticity.

The earlier research below remains historical. No blanket claim is made that all fees, offices or requirements have been reverified in this update.

## Scope and principal findings

LebFlow can provide a useful preparation and tracking experience across several Lebanese administrative services, but the evidence does not support a single nationwide document checklist or appointment calendar. A reliable guide must identify the particular procedure, applicant category, receiving authority, and source version. Application, renewal, replacement, authentication, equivalence, and professional registration should remain distinct procedures even when presented under the same service category.

This report covers publicly accessible guidance reviewed on 13 September 2026, principally for applications made inside Lebanon. Official Lebanese authority pages are the preferred sources. LibanPost is treated as a service provider describing its own submission routes, rather than as the issuer of the underlying documents. Undated pages and inaccessible attachments are identified explicitly. The review date records inspection, not the effective date of a government rule.

The strongest usable findings concern passport identity-document rules, the distinction between passport processing times and appointment availability, judicial-record submission routes, and the published reception arrangements for university authentication. Driving-license attachments remain less certain because the TMO portal did not expose a readable checklist or live appointment inventory. Municipality and professional categories need more specific subservices before their document lists can become authoritative.

The application therefore presents source-backed core guidance where available, and partial-verification labels elsewhere. This is a publication decision based on the evidence, not a representation of official approval. A successful registration form, an attached scan, a prepared checklist, and a government-issued document are four different events.

| Service | Supported information | Important unresolved information | Appropriate frontend behavior |

The underlying administrative evidence is discussed below with direct references. The “appropriate frontend behavior” column is product analysis.

## Passport applications and renewals

### Identity and document preparation

General Security’s passport page identifies an approved A4 application, identity evidence, a recent certified portrait, and the previous passport when available. It also describes additional parental or guardianship procedures for applicants under 18 and A4 copies of attachments. These conditions justify explicit conditional items in the guide instead of making parental consent a requirement for all applicants.[^1]

The dedicated identity table is material to personalization. It distinguishes identity-card age and likeness and specifies cases involving a recent individual extract and additional identity evidence. Its replacement section describes a matching ID or a matching new-format individual extract issued within the stated one-year period. The receiving route is part of that table, so the nearest geographical office should not automatically be presented as the correct receiving office.[^2]

The renewal interface should ask whether the person has a biometric passport, whether it is available, and whether the request is an ordinary replacement or a loss/damage case. The ordinary-replacement page describes remaining validity below 18 months and lists a particular set of documents. That should be read together with the dedicated identity table, rather than silently assuming every old page contains the complete current rules.[^3]

For example, the question “My passport expires in four months; what do I need?” can route to replacement guidance. It does not establish the applicant’s age, identity-card condition, place of application, or whether the passport is lost. A future personalized guide should collect these missing facts before presenting a definitive checklist. This is a proposed decision flow, not a determination of any individual’s eligibility.

### Conflicting official text

The Arabic general passport page’s displayed photo dimensions differ from the English general page and from the Arabic replacement page. The English page also describes copying nonblank passport pages, while the Arabic general page describes a copy of the particulars page. The report does not resolve these inconsistencies by choosing a language version without further evidence.[^1][^3][^4]

The current frontend consequently asks the applicant to confirm photograph dimensions before printing. That is a more faithful representation of the evidence than repeating an apparently exact measurement that may come from a stale, mistranscribed, or differently scoped page. A production knowledge base should retain both claims, identify the discrepancy, and record the authority’s resolution when obtained.

### Fees, processing, and appointments

The dedicated fee/processing page lists base fees of LBP 6,000,000 for five years and LBP 10,000,000 for ten years. It distinguishes ordinary regional processing at 30 working days, urgent regional processing at seven working days, and urgent Public Relations processing at 48 hours, with extra paid services for urgent routes. These are published values, not a personalized payable total or delivery guarantee.[^5]

A processing duration begins at a different point from the wait for an appointment. Nothing in that table identifies the next available appointment. LebFlow should not add a published duration to today’s date and label the result as a bookable slot. The General Security contact-center page provides the official 1717 contact route for confirmation.[^6]

## Judicial records

ISF’s guidance provides identification alternatives for Lebanese residents and describes personal submission, legal representation, a mukhtar-certified mandate, and LibanPost. It separately addresses institutional requests and applicants abroad or of another nationality. The institutional route includes a letter identifying the person responsible for receiving the records; that attachment must not be imposed on an ordinary individual request.[^7]

LibanPost’s police-record page describes a route for Lebanese citizens, including personal submission and parental submission for minor children. It states that the provider processes the application with the concerned authority and delivers the resulting record. These provider instructions should not replace the authority’s rules for a different route.[^8]

The frontend adds conditional authorization, residence, and institution-letter items. It does not assign a universal fee or a fabricated appointment. A future guide should first distinguish a Lebanese resident applying personally, a representative, a foreign resident, an applicant abroad, or an institutional request, then select only the relevant attachments.

Until that personalized decision flow is implemented, conditional items can be explicitly marked “does not apply” and restored. They are counted separately from prepared documents, so excluding a condition does not falsely claim that its evidence was collected.

The word “immediate” in a description of document issuance after completion also cannot establish that the applicant will be admitted at an arbitrary time. Reception hours, queue conditions, and completion of the application remain separate questions. The prototype uses an official-channel link and a preferred-date planner instead of a “confirmed booking” badge.

## Driving licenses

The official TMO address redirected to its web portal but did not provide a readable current checklist or live slot data in the accessible content. This is an access limitation, not evidence that the service is closed or that no appointments exist.[^9]

LibanPost lists selected registration-department services, including driving-license renewal and lost/damaged-license replacement. Its accessible description supports the existence of that provider route but does not provide the full current first-license document checklist, test eligibility, or fee table.[^10]

The frontend’s ID, application form, photos, medical/fitness certificate, and training/test entries therefore remain a preparation template requiring confirmation. Renewal substitutes the existing-license item for first-license training documents. This improves the interface without claiming that every listed document is officially required for every driver.

Before making this service authoritative, the data model needs license category, age eligibility, nationality/residence, first issuance versus renewal versus replacement, branch, required tests, medical validity, and payment details. The next research priority is obtaining current TMO-issued instructions through an accessible official channel. Historic traffic studies and informal social-media accounts should not fill those missing fields as if they were current operational rules.

## University issuance, authentication, and equivalence

Following the ministry’s service directory to its actual authentication page reveals a more specific service than “all university documents.” The page covers documents from legally licensed private higher-education institutions in Lebanon. It gives the first floor of the ministry, reception from 09:00 to 13:00, next-day collection, and the contact number 01 772531. It does not expose a complete current attachment list or a fee schedule on that page.[^11]

These published hours are useful to display but are not live availability. “Daily” reception text should not be converted into a seven-day calendar without checking official working days and exceptions. The interface asks users to confirm before traveling.

A university registrar issues transcripts or certificates; an authentication office authenticates eligible documents; an equivalence committee evaluates a different request. LebFlow should expose these as different future subservices. For now, the guide states its scope and marks identification, form, translation, and prior-attestation questions as matters to confirm.

The next evidence collection should identify required originals, certified copies, accepted university signatures, representative authorization, document language, intended destination, and any prior legalization. These are proposed research fields. They are not a claim that every authentication application requires all of them.

## Municipality and construction procedures

DGU’s new-building-permit page provides a concrete construction workflow with owner signatures, an engineer’s file, site and survey documentation, photographs, municipal technical conditions, a recent planning/easement certificate, and additional approvals for special project categories. Its instructions also address the competent receiving route and retaining the registration reference.[^12]

The frontend adds these as construction-only examples. It would be misleading to require engineering plans from someone seeking an unrelated municipal certificate. Before providing an exact local checklist, LebFlow needs the municipality, cadastral area, named procedure, property/representation status, and project type.

The accessible DGU page also contains amounts and a stated processing duration, but it has no clear current update date. Those numbers are not used as today’s municipal fee quote or guaranteed deadline. More recent local notices or direct authority confirmation are needed. Preserving the detailed source while withholding an unverified price is an intentional evidence distinction.

## Professional documents and engineering

MEHE’s engineering-practice page describes an application route through Public Works, or Agriculture for agricultural engineering. Its linked attachment page could not be retrieved. A listed weekly committee meeting should not be interpreted as a weekly public appointment slot.[^13]

The Beirut engineers’ order’s internal rules describe a registration framework and supporting evidence of the statutory conditions. This supports distinguishing practice permission from order registration, but does not establish one attachment list for all professions.[^14]

The frontend accordingly adds a practice-permission item, with qualifications and membership evidence clearly scoped. Current profession-specific checklists, recognized qualifications, equivalence requirements, responsible body, fees, and appointment arrangements still require verification before the category can become a comprehensive service.

## Appointment design

### Four distinct states

LebFlow should distinguish the following concepts in both interface text and stored data:

| Concept | Meaning | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Published opening hours | When an office says it receives visitors | Dated office guidance and current exceptions |
| Preferred date | The visitor’s own plan | User selection only |
| Available slot | Capacity currently exposed by a provider | Live slot response with service, branch, timezone, and expiry |
| Confirmed booking | Reservation accepted by the provider | Provider confirmation/reference and booking status |

Only preferred dates are stored by this frontend. They can be created, edited, persisted with a personal case, displayed on the appointments page, and removed. The UI explicitly states that removal does not cancel a government booking. Dates and times are interpreted in Asia/Beirut and past or invalid selections are rejected.

No live integration was established for any of the six categories. An “availability unknown” result must not be displayed as “no appointments available.” One expresses missing information; the other asserts that a real inventory was checked and found empty.

### Future live integration

A production connector should receive a service identifier, an eligible receiving office, applicant category, and time range. A returned slot should have a provider-generated identifier, start/end time, timezone, retrieval timestamp, and expiry. Confirmation must recheck availability because another visitor may take the slot between viewing and booking.

Recommended booking states are requested, pending provider confirmation, confirmed, rejected, and cancelled, with the local preferred-date planner kept separate. Network timeouts and lost responses need reconciliation against the provider reference. A request should never silently change from “preferred” to “confirmed” because its date was saved successfully in LebFlow.

The evidence reviewed here does not establish a public, supported booking API. A suitable next step is identifying authorized integration channels per authority or provider. Requiring access to an API is a functional dependency, not a reason to show invented availability in the prototype.

## Registration and identity documents

### Implemented frontend contract

The registration screen requires full name, date of birth, ID number, email, a password and matching confirmation, an ID image, an individual civil-status extract, and acknowledgement of the preview behavior. These are LebFlow’s requested registration fields. They are not presented as a government rule that every service requires both an ID card and an extract.

The ID image accepts JPEG, PNG, or WebP. The extract also permits PDF. Both are limited to nonempty files of at most 5 MiB. The UI performs basic type and signature checks, displays image previews, supports replacement/removal, and blocks completion when an attachment is missing or invalid. These checks detect common accidental mistakes; they do not establish authenticity, legibility, or absence of malicious content.

The prototype creates a session-only profile and labels identity as unverified. It makes no upload request, saves no password, and keeps profile details and File objects out of localStorage and sessionStorage. Refreshing or signing out clears the profile. Anonymous case progress and language preferences remain separate browser-local data, not authenticated account storage.

### Production requirements

OWASP recommends layered upload controls: allowlisted formats, independent content/type checks, limits, application-generated filenames, restricted storage, authorization, and malware/content processing where appropriate. A signature test alone is insufficient. These checks need to be repeated on the server because client validation can be bypassed.[^15]

A production implementation should use real authentication and account recovery, server-enforced access controls, verified contact channels, and protected session handling. OWASP’s authentication guidance is a relevant implementation baseline; the preview password rule is not a claim of production authentication compliance.[^16]

For LebFlow specifically, account ownership, document storage, identity review, and service eligibility should have separate records. A document’s “attached,” “review pending,” “accepted,” and “rejected” states must not be collapsed into “verified citizen.” Access to one person’s files must not follow from knowing their ID number. Retention and deletion behavior must be decided before collecting real identity records.

## Arabic, English, and French

The application translates navigation, search, service descriptions, requirements, fees, source labels, appointment controls, request stages, registration labels, errors, and empty states. Arabic uses right-to-left document direction; names, identifiers, filenames, and email addresses are isolated where needed. HTML language and direction are separate settings, consistent with W3C internationalization guidance.[^17]

Changing the language must preserve the current request, document progress, preferred date, and registration draft. Stored request stages use stable values rather than translated labels. Search includes all three language versions, so changing interface language does not invalidate the service category or require recreating a case.

Translations describe the same source-backed or provisional status. A confident Arabic label must not conceal a caveat shown in English. Administrative wording should receive professional bilingual review before production, especially where the source itself differs across languages. Interface translation does not certify the translation of an official uploaded document.

## Knowledge-base and future assistant requirements

The recommended knowledge record is a scoped claim, not an undifferentiated web-page excerpt. Each record should identify authority, service/subservice, jurisdiction, applicant conditions, source URL, source language, publication/effective dates when known, review date, and confidence or verification state. Conflicting and superseded claims should remain traceable.

For a future RAG assistant, retrieval should filter by the applicant’s actual route before generating a checklist. Missing mandatory facts should lead to a short clarifying question. A retrieved old fee should not be emitted as a current price; a processing-duration sentence should not answer a nearest-appointment question. Responses should cite the exact page supporting the relevant condition.

The current frontend’s source records and three-language content are a starting point for this structure. No AI assistant, backend knowledge service, identity verification, notification delivery, or government booking connector is implemented by this frontend update. These are separate implementation workstreams with distinct acceptance criteria.
